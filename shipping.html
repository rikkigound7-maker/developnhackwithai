What you receive

Every order is delivered as an sk- API key for https://freeai.jembatanai.com, with the plan and period you bought. A renewal of an existing key extends that key, with no new key.

How delivery works

  • Payment confirmed — you arrange the payment on Telegram. We accept all payment methods as well as crypto.
  • Order bound to Telegram — open the claim link from the checkout page in @HacknDevelopWithAi. It binds the order to your Telegram account.
  • Key delivered — the bot sends the key to your Telegram chat automatically, with no manual step by an admin.

A paid order that has not been claimed yet stays on file. The key is sent as soon as you open the claim link.

Shipping cost

Shipping costs $0. The total is the plan price, and Develop & Hack_With_Ai adds no other fee. Any fee charged by a payment provider is shown before you pay.

Service area

The key works anywhere with internet access. Nothing is shipped to a physical address.

Billing address

We may ask for your name and contact details. They are used to verify the payment and to contact you about the order, not as a shipping address. The details are in the privacy policy.

If the key has not arrived

  • Check your chat with @HacknDevelopWithAi and make sure the claim link was opened.
  • Contact support on Telegram with your order number.
  • A paid order we cannot deliver is settled under the refund policy.
