Questions about pricing

Buying, renewing, limits.

Which payment methods work?
Contact us on Telegram to pay and buy. We accept UPI, credit card, debit card and QR code.
Is there a free trial?
Yes: 500K tokens once per Telegram account, claimed in the bot. It opens the all 19 Pro models.
What is the difference between the levels?
Only the models, parallel sessions and RPM. Pro is $40 and opens the 19-model Pro pool, with 4 sessions and 60 requests/min. Max is $55 and adds the 7-model Max pool (26 models total), 8 sessions and 120 requests/min. Ultra is $20 a day or $100 a month and adds the 5-model Ultra pool (all 31 models), 14 sessions and 240 requests/min.
Can I buy Pro or Max daily?
No. Pro and Max are monthly only. Ultra can be bought daily or monthly.
Are tokens or requests capped?
Pro, Max and Ultra have no token quota or total request cap. RPM is 60 requests/min for Pro, 120 for Max and 240 for Ultra; parallel sessions are 4, 8 and 14.
How do I renew?
Renew in the Telegram bot. A renewal keeps the same key, so your client configuration does not change.
What happens when a Token Plan runs out?
Buy another Token Plan in the bot and the same key is active again at once. The remaining balance carries into the new plan.
