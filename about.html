What we do

Hack & Develop With Ai is an AI API gateway. It puts one OpenAI-compatible and Anthropic-compatible endpoint in front of 16 models from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, DeepSeek and Moonshot. Instead of buying and managing a separate key from every provider, you buy one sk- key and call every model through the same base URL: https://freeai.hackdevelopwithai.com/v1.

The gateway is built for coding tools. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Cline, OpenCode and Kilo Code all work by changing the base URL and the API key. The model name selects the model; the plan selects parallel sessions and the RPM limit.

How it works

  1. Pick a plan: Pro, Max or Ultra, billed monthly, or daily on Ultra.
  2. Pay on Telegram by UPI, credit card, debit card or QR code.
  3. The bot delivers your sk- key automatically after the payment is confirmed.
  4. Point your client at the base URL and send requests. GET /v1/models lists every model your key can use.

Models and compatibility

Every key opens the four base models: Claude Haiku 5, DeepSeek V4 Pro, GPT-6 Luna and GPT-6 Terra. Max adds eight extension models, including Claude Sonnet 5.5, Claude Sonnet 5, GPT-6.1 Sol, Gemini 4 Argon, GPT-6 Sol, Kimi K3 and Kimi K3.1. Ultra adds the frontier set: Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6 Astra, Claude Opus 5 and Claude Fable 5.1.

The gateway speaks the OpenAI format through /v1/chat/completions and /v1/responses, and the Anthropic format through /v1/messages. The same key works with both. See the model catalogue for every model and its id.

Plans and pricing

Pro is $30 a month with 4 parallel sessions and 4 base models (60 requests/min). Max is $55 a month with 8 parallel sessions and 12 models (120 requests/min). Ultra is $20 a day or $100 a month with 14 parallel sessions and all 16 models (240 requests/min). Pro and Max are monthly only. Token Plans add a 30-day balance, and pay-per-use uses a dollar balance that does not expire.

Prices are in US dollars. The site can display rupees at a fixed ₹95 per $1 for convenience; the order is charged in US dollars. See plans and pricing for the full comparison, or read what unlimited tokens and requests mean.

Payments and delivery

Payments are handled on Telegram. We accept UPI, credit card, debit card and QR code. Delivery is digital: the bot sends the key automatically once the payment is confirmed, so there is no shipping and no physical product. A 500K-token trial is available once per Telegram account on the base models.

Who it is for

Indie developers, students, freelancers and small teams who use AI coding assistants and want predictable pricing instead of per-token surprises. It also suits developers in India who want UPI payment and a rupee view of the price.
