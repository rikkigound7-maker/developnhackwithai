Summary

We do not sell customer data

Data is disclosed only where applicable law requires it.

We do not train models on your data

Our systems have no training pipeline and no export of conversation data.

Conversation content goes to the model providers

That is the only way your request can be answered. On their side, their policies apply.

Recent content is kept briefly for diagnosis

Only the most recent requests, each part truncated, then overwritten by the requests that follow.

Who else receives data

Only the parties needed to answer your requests and deliver your key.

  • Third-party model providers — the content of your request, so that the model can answer. Confidentiality on their side follows their policies.
  • Telegram — the bot and the Mini App run on Telegram, so messages to the bot pass through Telegram's servers.
  • Sentry — technical error reports. Chat request bodies are dropped, and authentication headers and cookies are filtered out.
  • Cloudflare — this website is served through Cloudflare. API requests to freeai.jembatanai.com are not.

This website

This website stores no orders, keys or balances.

No cookies and no third-party analytics. Display preferences (your theme choice) are kept in your own browser.

There is no self-service way to delete or download your data yet. For questions or requests about your data, contact the admin on Telegram.
