Questions about pricing

Buying, renewing, limits.

Which payment methods work?
Contact us on Telegram to pay and buy. We accept UPI, credit card, debit card and QR code.
Is there a free trial?
Yes: 500K tokens once per Telegram account, claimed in the bot. It opens the eight base models.
What is the difference between the levels?
Only the models and the number of parallel sessions. Pro has 8 base models and 2 sessions, Max adds 5 extension models and 6 sessions, Ultron adds the frontier brands and 14 sessions.
Are tokens or requests capped?
No. Pro, Max and Ultron have no token quota, no request quota and no per-minute limit. The only limit is parallel sessions.
How do I renew?
Renew in the Telegram bot. A renewal keeps the same key, so your client configuration does not change.
What happens when a Token Plan runs out?
Buy another Token Plan in the bot and the same key is active again at once. The remaining balance carries into the new plan.
