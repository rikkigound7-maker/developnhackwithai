1. Licence: one key, one person

An API key is a personal licence for one person. It may be used on that person's own devices and in any editor. A key shared by several people, published, or resold without a Partner licence is revoked without refund.

2. Personal gateways are fully allowed

You may put your key behind a personal gateway such as LiteLLM, as long as you are the only person using it. A gateway that serves more than one person is not allowed.

6. Fair use

The Pro, Max and Ultron plans have no token quota and no rate limit; the only limit is the number of parallel sessions for the level. A request above it waits a moment for its turn, then gets a 429 to retry if the queue has not opened yet. Trial and legacy keys carry their own limits.

7. Revocation and appeals

A key that breaches these terms is revoked without refund. If you believe a decision was wrong, file an appeal with the first 8 characters of your key. An accepted appeal reactivates the key automatically; a rejection is final and states its reason.

8. Reselling

Reselling a key is not permitted. Each key is a personal licence for one person.

9. Data and privacy

We store the usage metadata required for billing, quota and abuse prevention. Conversation content is forwarded to third-party model providers so that your request can be answered. Card details are entered on the payment provider page, never pass through our servers, and are not stored by us. We do not sell customer data.
